LEARNING BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

A valuable step from study to work

Industrial training gives students the chance to experience a professional environment, practise their skills, and learn from people already working in their field. It can be an exciting time, but keeping everyone informed and focused takes good communication.

SIWES360 is being built to support that process. It brings the people involved in a student’s training into one shared experience, helping make the journey clearer from the first day on placement to the lessons students take back to their studies.

ONE JOURNEY, SHARED SUPPORT

Made for the people involved

Each person has a different part to play in a successful training experience. SIWES360 is designed with those perspectives in mind.

Students

Build confidence in the workplace, connect classroom knowledge to real tasks, and make the most of each learning opportunity.

Industry supervisors

Guide students through day-to-day workplace learning and help them develop practical experience and professional habits.

University supervisors

Stay connected to students’ industrial training and support the link between academic learning and workplace experience.
